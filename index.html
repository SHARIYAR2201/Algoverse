                + Big-O vs Big-Θ vs Big-Ω caveats
            
            

                Big-O is an asymptotic upper bound: f(n) ≤ c·g(n) for large n. Big-Ω is a lower bound, and Big-Θ means both hold (a tight bound). Best, average and worst case are separate questions from O/Θ/Ω. The curves plot idealized operation counts for tiny n; they are not runtimes, and constants, hardware and caches change real measurements.
